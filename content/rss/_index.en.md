## What is RSS?

**RSS** is a format for receiving updates from websites. You subscribe to a
feed and get notified about new notes automatically.

## How to subscribe

1. **Install an RSS reader:**
   - **Cross-platform:** [Feedly](https://feedly.com), [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com)
   - **iOS/macOS:** [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com), [Reeder](https://reederapp.com)
   - **Android:** [Feeder](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.nononsenseapps.feeder/), [FeedMe](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.seazon.feedme)
   - **Desktop:** [Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net), [Fluent Reader](https://hyliu.me/fluent-reader/)
   - **Browser:** [Feedbro](https://nodetics.com/feedbro/) (Chrome/Firefox)

2. **Copy the feed URL** (use the "Copy" button above).

3. **Add it to your reader** — done!

---

💡 **Tip:** Subscribe only to the sections you care about to keep your feed
focused.
