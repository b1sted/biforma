Welcome to the **Biforma** example site. This site is intentionally small, but it
covers the theme's two layout modes and most of its content components.

Start with the [Introduction](/introduction/) for a quick tour, browse the
[Guides](/guides/) section to see the tree-view navigation in action, or open the
[Feature Showcase](/guides/advanced/feature-showcase/) for a full styling
reference.

{{< admonition type="tip" title="Try both modes" >}}
The site ships with `themeMode = "docs"`. Change it to `"blog"` in
`exampleSite/hugo.toml` to preview the bento post feed and profile sidebar.
{{< /admonition >}}
